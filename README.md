# 🕯️ Whispers Between Worlds

> **Some stories are forgotten. Some memories refuse to disappear.**

**Whispers Between Worlds** is a 2D narrative mystery/horror game set inside an abandoned attic filled with forgotten objects, hidden spaces, and mysterious connections to other worlds.
What initially appears to be an ordinary attic slowly reveals itself to be something much stranger.
An **old book** and a **mysterious teddy bear** seem to hold connections to worlds beyond reality. As the player explores the attic and interacts with these objects, fragments of forgotten stories begin to surface.
And somewhere between the whispers, there is a story waiting to be completed...

---

## 📖 Story

An abandoned attic sits untouched, filled with dusty furniture, forgotten belongings, old storage boxes, and objects from another time.
Among them are two objects that seem strangely different from everything else:
📕 **An Old Book**
🧸 **A Mysterious Teddy Bear**
The objects are not simply remnants of the past.
They are **connections to other worlds**.
When the player interacts with them, the boundaries between the attic and these mysterious worlds begin to disappear.
Each world contains fragments of a forgotten story — memories, clues, characters, and events that seem to be connected in ways the player does not initially understand.
Throughout the attic, faint whispers can occasionally be heard.
> *"The story is not finished..."*
The player must explore, investigate, and uncover the truth hidden behind these objects.

---

# 🏚️ The Attic Hub

The **Attic** is the central hub of *Whispers Between Worlds* and the player's main exploration area.
It acts as the connection point between the ordinary world and the mysterious worlds hidden within the objects.
The attic is designed to encourage **exploration and observation**, with important story elements hidden within the environment.

---

# 🎮 Gameplay

The core gameplay focuses on **exploration, environmental storytelling, and mystery**.

### The player can:

* Explore the abandoned attic
* Move between connected rooms and passages
* Investigate mysterious objects
* Interact with important story elements
* Discover hidden connections between objects and worlds
* Enter mysterious story worlds
* Search environments for clues
* Uncover fragments of forgotten stories
* Experience atmospheric mystery and horror

Rather than relying purely on traditional combat, the game focuses on making the player **curious about what happened**.
Every object may have a story.
Every room may contain a clue.
And some whispers may be trying to guide the player somewhere.

---

# 🌍 Connected Worlds

The attic contains objects that act as gateways to completely different worlds.
Each world has its own atmosphere, story, and mystery while remaining connected to the central narrative.

---

## 📕 World I — The Forgotten Princess

The **Old Book** is connected to a mysterious fantasy world containing the story of a forgotten princess.
When the player enters the book's world, they find themselves in a once-beautiful kingdom that has fallen strangely silent.
The kingdom appears abandoned.
The castle gates stand open.
The streets are empty.
There are no celebrations, no guards, and no people.
Only traces of a story that seems to have been interrupted.
A mysterious whisper follows the player:
> **"The story is not finished..."**

### 👑 The Mystery

Long ago, the kingdom was preparing for a great celebration surrounding its princess.
But before the celebration could take place, the princess disappeared.
No one knows what happened to her.
Some believe she escaped.
Some believe she was taken.
Others believe the kingdom itself has forgotten her.
The player must explore the silent kingdom and uncover fragments of the princess's story.
Pages, objects, environmental clues, and remnants of memories gradually reveal what happened.
As the player progresses, the world begins to feel increasingly unnatural.
Familiar places appear different.
Objects seem to move.
Fragments of conversations can be heard.
And the player starts to question whether they are discovering the princess's story...
or becoming part of it.

### 🔎 Player's Objective

The player must:
* Explore the forgotten kingdom
* Search for clues about the princess
* Discover fragments of her story
* Investigate the abandoned castle
* Understand why the kingdom was forgotten
* Piece together what happened before the story ended
The ultimate mystery is not simply **"Where is the princess?"**
It is:
> **"Why was her story forgotten?"**
The player's discoveries within this world contribute to the larger mystery of *Whispers Between Worlds*.

---

## 🧸 World II — The Teddy Bear World

The mysterious **Teddy Bear** leads to a much darker and more psychological world.
Unlike the fantasy setting of the Forgotten Princess, this world focuses on **memories, emotions, loneliness, and things that people try to forget**.
The environment gradually becomes unsettling as the player explores.
Familiar objects may appear in unfamiliar places.
Memories may repeat themselves.
And the player begins to encounter fragments of emotions connected to the teddy bear.
The deeper the player explores, the more they begin to understand that the teddy bear may not simply be an object.
It may be connected to someone who desperately wanted to hold on to a memory.

### 🧠 Themes

The Teddy Bear World explores themes such as:
* Forgotten memories
* Childhood
* Loneliness
* Attachment
* Loss
* Fear
* Emotional memories

The world is designed to create a more psychological form of horror rather than relying only on traditional scares.

---

# 🔗 The Connection Between Worlds

Although the worlds appear completely different, they are connected through the attic.
The attic acts as the **anchor point** between them.
The objects found inside the attic are more than gateways — they are pieces of a larger mystery.

---

# 🕯️ Atmosphere & Visual Style

*Whispers Between Worlds* uses a **2D pixel-art visual style** with a dark, atmospheric mystery/horror presentation.

### 🎨 Visual Direction

The visual design aims to create a feeling of **quiet mystery rather than constant horror**.
The player should feel like something is wrong even when nothing is happening.
